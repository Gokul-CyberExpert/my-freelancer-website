QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working together and billing timelines.

What is your typical project timeline? expand_more
Standard landing pages and brand websites generally take 2 to 3 weeks from initial kickoff to live launch. Complex mobile apps or multi-featured platforms typically range between 4 to 8 weeks depending on backend and integration needs.
How do we handle payments and invoicing? expand_more
Projects are typically split into standard milestone invoices: 50% upfront deposit upon contract signing, and 50% upon final delivery and client acceptance before DNS transfer. Custom installment schedules are available for larger scopes.
Can you build both the design and the engineering? expand_more
Yes. I handle both UI design and technical frontend/backend development end-to-end. If you already have existing Figma or design specifications, I can also take those and execute them with 100% fidelity into clean code.
What happens after the project is deployed? expand_more
Every completed project includes 30 days of complimentary post-launch bug fixing and tech support. Following that, you can retain me for on-demand feature additions, maintenance retainers, or continuous performance audits.
